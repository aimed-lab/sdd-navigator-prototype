Molecular Glue Degraders in Oncology

Drug Discovery Proteomics Oncology

summarize Episode Summary

In this deep-dive session, Dr. Helena Vance discusses the emerging paradigm of Molecular Glue Degraders (MGDs), specifically focusing on their application in treating refractory liquid tumors.

  • Overview of the Proximity-Inducing Pharmacology framework.
  • Analysis of Thalidomide analogs and their evolution into precise degraders.
  • Structural biology insights: How MGDs reprogram E3 ligase surface topology.
  • Clinical trial updates for CRBN-mediated degradation in AML.

Key Concepts

Induced Proximity

The mechanism of bringing two proteins together that do not naturally interact to trigger a biological response.

E3 Ligase Reprogramming

Modifying the substrate specificity of an E3 ligase to target 'undruggable' proteins for degradation.

Molecular Glues vs PROTACs

A comparison of monovalent versus bivalent degrader architectures and their pharmacokinetic profiles.

Ternary Complex Stability

Understanding the cooperative binding kinetics required for efficient protein ubiquitination.
